Public availability and checksums do not establish commercial permission. Do not
assume an Open Government Licence applies to an unrelated artefact. Before any
production import, an accountable owner must record the applicable written
permission or exact dataset licence, allowed storage/display/modification,
attribution, retention and any onward-use restrictions. Provider sites and
provincial sources require their own review. Facts and protected expression must
be distinguished rather than assuming a blanket licence or blanket prohibition.

No source rights were newly granted by this code package. Free display in a
commercial product is not automatically noncommercial use. Official source
handoffs and truthful unavailable states remain valid product behavior.

Collection stays operator controlled, obeys fixed URLs and robots/access limits,
and stops on denial, timeout or format change. No bypass, scheduler, production
startup import or infrastructure is introduced.
