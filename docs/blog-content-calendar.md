## Publishing checklist

- Use a specific, search-focused title and meta description.
- Lead with the client problem, then provide a short practical checklist.
- Link only to current, official sources for program facts and changing requirements.
- Add a NorthBridgeAI CTA that matches the article's user intent.
- Add the English and French routes to the generated sitemap.
- Share the article from the founder/company LinkedIn account with a concise, informational summary and no promise of outcomes.
