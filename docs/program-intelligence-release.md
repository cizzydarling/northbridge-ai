# Bounded program intelligence

This package supplies original EN/FR preparation summaries for CEC, FSW and FST,
alongside the existing eleven PNP-jurisdiction references and separate Quebec
selection reference. It is not a complete eligibility engine or intake feed.

The three exact IRCC program sources are registered in `official_sources.py` and
referenced by `program_criteria.json`. Source review on 2026-10-30 does not establish
publication or legal effective dates; those remain unknown. Independent criteria
acceptance is pending. The November 3 review boundary is a review requirement, not
automatic immigration-rule activation or an asserted legal expiry date.

CEC Canadian-work, FSW primary-occupation and FST trade qualification checks remain
distinct. Criteria exceptions, language minima, funds and admissibility require
the applicable official source review. The FSW selection grid is not CRS. No user
facts, NOC suggestion or score cause an eligibility verdict.

Quebec never belongs to PNP or Express Entry selection. Nunavut has no PNP. A
reference's existence does not establish an open intake. Existing streams remain
under review until a separately reviewed, dated release supports their status.

No migration, scheduler, automatic source collection or production activation is
included. Any copied source expression or additional provincial dataset requires
its own reuse review. Extend provincial streams only through separately approved
source-referenced criteria and negative jurisdiction/intake tests.
